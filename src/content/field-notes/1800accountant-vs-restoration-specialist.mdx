## What 1-800Accountant Does Well

1-800Accountant built a large, recognizable business by solving a real problem for the smallest end of the market, and that's worth acknowledging plainly.

**It's genuinely affordable.** As of 2026, its bundled packages are commonly advertised in the range of roughly **$200–$400+ per month** depending on the bundle, frequently with promotional first-year pricing <Citation source="1-800Accountant" url="https://1800accountant.com/pricing" year="2026" />. For a very small business, getting bookkeeping, tax, and some advisory in one low monthly price is a strong value proposition.

**It bundles tax and a CPA.** You're not just getting bookkeeping — the package includes tax preparation and access to accounting professionals, including CPAs. For a freelancer or small LLC, having tax and books under one roof at this price is convenient.

**It's accessible.** The whole model is built around being easy to start, easy to afford, and available to businesses that might otherwise have no professional accounting help at all. There's real value in meeting small businesses where they are.

For a consultant, a single-truck trades business, an e-commerce side hustle, or a small LLC, 1-800Accountant is a reasonable, economical choice. The issue for restoration is structural, and it's worth being precise about.

## The Volume Model vs. a Specialized Trade

Here's the honest mechanics of how a service hits a low price point: **standardization at scale.**

Volume-priced accounting services keep costs down by making the work as repeatable as possible across thousands of similar small businesses. A simple LLC's books look a lot like the next simple LLC's books, so a standardized chart of accounts, a standardized categorization process, and a standardized close can be applied efficiently to all of them. That's a smart, legitimate business model — and it's exactly why the price can be low.

Restoration breaks the template. An insurance restoration contractor's books look nothing like a generic small LLC's:

- Revenue arrives **in stages from carriers**, not in clean monthly payments
- **Supplements** create approved-but-uncollected receivables that need lifecycle tracking
- **TPA fees** vary by program and need to be coded by carrier
- Costs split across **labor, materials, subcontractors, and equipment** at the job level
- **Reconstruction** introduces multi-month WIP

None of that fits a standardized small-business template — which means **the same standardization that makes the price low is what prevents the service from handling restoration.** You can't get specialized, non-standard accounting out of a model built specifically to avoid non-standard work. It's not that the people aren't capable; it's that the product isn't built for it.

## The Honest Bottom Line

1-800Accountant does what it says: affordable, bundled accounting for small businesses, with tax and a CPA in the mix. For a lot of very small operations, that's genuinely good value, and there's no shame in starting there.

But a restoration owner has to be clear-eyed about the trade. The low price is bought with standardization, and **restoration is the opposite of standard.** The supplements, the staged insurance payments, the TPA economics, the job-level margins — the parts that decide whether you're actually profitable — are precisely the parts a volume model is designed not to touch.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is 1-800Accountant good for restoration companies?

It's an affordable, accessible bundled service built for high volume — small LLCs, freelancers, sole proprietors. For restoration, the volume-priced model isn't designed to deliver the restoration-specific layer: Xactimate reconciliation, ACV/RCV staging, supplements, TPA fees, job costing, and WIP.

### How much does 1-800Accountant cost?

As of 2026, packages are commonly advertised in the roughly $200–$400+ per month range depending on the bundle, often with promotional first-year pricing — generally among the more affordable bundled options. A specialist costs more because the scope is larger. Confirm current pricing with the provider.

### What's the catch with a volume-priced service for restoration?

The low price comes from standardizing work across thousands of similar simple businesses. Restoration isn't simple or standard, so the model that makes it cheap is the same one that can't accommodate restoration's specialized accounting. It's a fit problem, not a quality problem.

### Does 1-800Accountant do job costing?

Its standard bundle is built around general small-business bookkeeping and tax, not project-based job costing. It doesn't assign costs to individual jobs, reconcile to Xactimate, or maintain a WIP schedule.
